About BHP

A Northern California integrator built around trust, responsiveness, and disciplined execution.

BHP Electrical Integrations is a Service-Disabled Veteran Owned Small Business specializing in low voltage, physical security, surveillance, and alarm projects for government, industrial, and commercial clients.

How We Work

Projects start with communication and end with a system teams can actually use.

BHP's messaging emphasizes open communication, relationship-driven delivery, and support from planning through a fully functional system. That approach carries through every engagement, whether the scope is a single upgrade or a multi-phase rollout.
