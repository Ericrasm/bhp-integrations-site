Frequently asked questions about working with BHP.

Get quick answers about project fit, service areas, certifications, and the kinds of systems BHP installs for commercial, industrial, and government clients.

What types of clients does BHP serve?

BHP focuses on commercial, industrial, and government clients across Northern California.

What systems do you install?

BHP installs low voltage infrastructure, physical security and access control, surveillance systems, and fire and burglar alarm systems.

Do you support public-sector procurement?

Yes. BHP is a Service-Disabled Veteran Owned Small Business and also carries SB/DVBE certification and active registration credentials.

How do projects usually start?

Most engagements begin with a conversation around site needs, project drawings, expansion plans, or retrofit requirements, followed by scoped planning and installation coordination.
