About Us

Built on Service.
Driven by Excellence.

BHP Electrical Integrations (operating as BHP Requiem, Inc.) is a Service-Disabled Veteran Owned Small Business headquartered in Northern California. We specialize in the full spectrum of security and low voltage services for government, industrial, and commercial clients.

Our team brings a military-grade commitment to every engagement — combining technical expertise with an unwavering dedication to client satisfaction. From small residential upgrades to large-scale government infrastructure projects, we deliver custom solutions that stand the test of time.

We believe great security starts with great relationships. That's why we maintain open lines of communication from the first consultation through final handover and beyond.
