Low Voltage Solutions

Professional network cabling and low voltage infrastructure for data, voice, and communications.

A reliable, high-performance low voltage infrastructure is the backbone of your organization's operations. BHP Integrations provides expert installation and termination of structured cabling systems — from Cat6 copper networks to multi-mode and single-mode fiber optic solutions — for indoor and outdoor applications. We handle everything from horizontal cabling and riser infrastructure to underground conduit work, ensuring your network is built to current TIA/EIA standards and ready to scale with your needs.

Cabling Services

  • Cat6 / Cat6A structured cabling
  • Multi-mode & single-mode fiber optic
  • Underground conduit installation
  • Network rack & patch panel builds
  • Cable testing & certification
  • Horizontal & backbone cabling

Additional Capabilities

  • Wireless AP & infrastructure cabling
  • VoIP & telephone cabling
  • A/V & control system wiring
  • Data center cabling & organization
  • TIA/EIA standards compliance
  • Documentation & as-built drawings

Physical Security & Access Control

Intelligent access control systems that give you complete command over who enters your facility and when.

Controlling access to your facility is one of the most important elements of any security strategy. BHP Integrations installs and integrates enterprise-grade access control systems that allow you to manage entry permissions electronically — from individual doors to entire campuses. Our solutions provide comprehensive audit trails with time-and-date stamped records, electronic scheduling, and user-friendly management dashboards for administrators and security personnel.

Access Control

  • Card reader & key fob systems
  • Biometric fingerprint / facial recognition
  • PIN pad & multi-factor auth
  • Electronic door strikes & mag-locks
  • Visitor management integration
  • Mobile credential support

Management Features

  • Centralized web management portal
  • Time-stamped audit logs & reports
  • Electronic scheduling & holidays
  • Real-time alerts & notifications
  • Multi-site management
  • API integration capabilities

Surveillance & CCTV Systems

Advanced video surveillance with AI analytics to proactively detect and respond to threats.

Modern surveillance goes far beyond passive recording. BHP Integrations designs and deploys intelligent camera systems that leverage AI-powered video analytics to detect suspicious activity, monitor perimeters, and alert security personnel in real time. We offer flexible storage options — cloud-based, on-premises NVR/DVR, or hybrid — tailored to your retention requirements and budget. Every installation is customized to provide optimal coverage with minimal blind spots.

Camera Systems

  • IP & analog HD cameras
  • PTZ (pan-tilt-zoom) cameras
  • License plate recognition (LPR)
  • Thermal & low-light cameras
  • Outdoor & vandal-resistant housings
  • 4K & multi-megapixel options

Analytics & Storage

  • AI-powered motion & object detection
  • Facial recognition analytics
  • Cloud-based video storage
  • On-premises NVR/DVR systems
  • Remote viewing via mobile & web
  • Customizable retention policies

Alarm Systems

Comprehensive fire and burglar alarm solutions with UL-certified monitoring for 24/7 protection.

A properly designed alarm system is your last line of defense against break-ins, fires, and other emergencies. BHP Integrations installs scalable fire and burglar alarm systems — from simple single-panel systems to complex networked installations spanning multiple buildings. All our alarm deployments are backed by UL-certified central station monitoring services, ensuring your property receives around-the-clock professional protection and rapid emergency response coordination.

Fire Alarm Systems

  • Addressable & conventional panels
  • Smoke, heat & CO detectors
  • Pull stations & notification appliances
  • NFPA 72 compliant installations
  • Annual inspection & testing
  • Integration with building automation

Burglar Alarm Systems

  • Motion detectors & glass break sensors
  • Door & window contact sensors
  • UL-certified 24/7 monitoring
  • Keypad & mobile arming/disarming
  • Audible & silent alarm options
  • Duress & panic buttons

General Electrical Services

Full-spectrum electrical contracting for commercial and residential projects — including smart technology integration.

Smart Technology
