Our Mission

Built on Service. Driven by Excellence.

BHP Electrical Integrations (operating as BHP Requiem, Inc.) is a Service-Disabled Veteran Owned Small Business headquartered in Clayton, California. Founded by a U.S. veteran, our company was established with a clear mission: to bring military-grade discipline and precision to the security and low voltage industry.

We serve government agencies, industrial facilities, and commercial businesses throughout Northern California, delivering custom solutions that combine technical expertise with open, transparent communication. Every project is approached with the same commitment to excellence that our founders carried into military service.

From the initial consultation to the final walk-through, we prioritize understanding your unique needs and building solutions that protect what matters most to you. Our clients aren't just customers — they're long-term partners whom we are honored to serve.

Core Values

What We Stand For

Our values aren't just words on a wall — they're the principles that guide every decision we make and every project we deliver.

Integrity

We do what we say we'll do, every time. Honest pricing, transparent timelines, and no surprises — our reputation is built on trust earned one project at a time.

Excellence

Mediocrity has no place in security. We hold ourselves to the highest standards of workmanship, using only quality equipment and following industry best practices.

Service

Service is in our DNA. From our military roots to the way we approach every client interaction, we are dedicated to serving our community and the people who trust us.

Innovation
